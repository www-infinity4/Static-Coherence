# Static-Coherence

This repository intentionally uses a **safety-first, evidence-based** scope.

## Scope
- Discusses physics and materials topics at a high level.
- Avoids operational guidance for weaponizable systems (including nuclear propulsion/reactor construction).
- Treats extraordinary claims (for example: “infinite energy channels,” Roswell reverse-engineering narratives, or static-field miracle alloys) as unverified unless supported by reproducible peer-reviewed evidence.

## Safety Policy
If prompted for speculative or hazardous engineering instructions, responses should:
1. Decline actionable design guidance.
2. Provide concise safety rationale.
3. Redirect to legitimate, non-harmful educational alternatives (e.g., established plasma physics, aerospace materials science, and reactor safety fundamentals).
